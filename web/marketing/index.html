How it works

One moment per run.

Three seconds after you stop, a card turns over. Everything else is quieter than that.

Run

No start button needed. Flying Cobra notices a run on its own, measures it, and stays out of your way. There's a big Start for people who like intent.

Reveal

The card slides up, pauses, and flips. Which animal you get depends on the run you did — where, when, how far, how steady.

Some animals only come out at night.

Collect

Earn the same animal again and your bond grows: baby, young, adult. Your collection fills in one wild corner of India at a time.

Trust

Real cards have serial numbers.

Anyone can fake a card image. Nobody can fake a serial number that lives in the Flying Cobra ledger. Type one in and you'll see that single card — the animal, the number, who earned it and when — or you'll learn that someone is bluffing.

Also accepts “tiger 427” or “TGR-0427”. Only serials are searchable — never people.

Privacy

Private by design.

Your settings read like a receipt of what we hold and why. Nothing is shown that you didn't choose to show.

  • Posters never show your route or start pointShare the animal and the serial. Your streets stay yours.
  • Age and gender are optional — and never shownOnly used to grade effort fairly. Stored as a birth year, nothing more.
  • Each permission asks separately, with a reasonLocation, motion, heart rate, notifications: one at a time, each skippable. “Not now” is as big as “Allow”.
  • India DPDP-awareBuilt for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act: purpose-limited, plain-language consent, export anytime.
  • Delete everything, anytimeOne tap in the trust centre removes your account, runs and cards. No emails to write.
