One moment per run.

Three seconds after you stop, a card turns over. Everything else in the app is quieter than that.

You run.

No start button needed. Flying Cobra notices a run on its own, measures it and stays out of your way. A big Start exists for people who like intent.

It turns over.

Which animal you get depends on the run you did: where, when, how far, how steady. Earn the same animal again and your bond grows, from baby to young to adult.

Some animals only come out at night.

Your pace picks the animal, not the prize.

Fast and slow are different families, never high and low tiers. Same foil, same celebration, same card weight.

The hour you run changes what you find.

Dawn has its birds and night has its own. Run before sunrise or after dark and a different bag opens.

A rooster on a branch at dawn becomes an owl as the sky turns to night. Your browser cannot show the 3D scene.

Every place you run leaves you something to keep.

A new state gives you its animal. A full week gives you a rarer one. Cross India over three weeks and a bird that crosses it too turns up.

Real cards have serial numbers.

Anyone can fake a card image. Nobody can fake a serial that lives in the Flying Cobra ledger. Type one in and you see that single card, or you learn that someone is bluffing.

Also accepts "tiger 427" or "TGR-0427". Only serials are searchable, never people.

Private by design.

Your settings read like a receipt of what we hold and why. Nothing is shown that you did not choose to show.

  • Posters never show your route or start point.Share the animal and the serial. Your streets stay yours.
  • Age and gender are optional, and never shown.Used only to grade effort fairly. Stored as a birth year, nothing more.
  • Each permission asks separately, with a reason.Location, motion, heart rate, notifications: one at a time, each skippable. "Not now" is as big as "Allow".
  • Built for India's data protection law.Purpose-limited, plain-language consent, and an export of everything whenever you ask.
  • Delete everything, anytime.One tap in the trust centre removes your account, your runs and your cards. No emails to write.

Your first run opens the bag.

Free. Works with location alone. Everything else is optional and asked for separately.
