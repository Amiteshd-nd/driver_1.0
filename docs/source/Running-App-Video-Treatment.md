# Running App — Director's Marketing Video Treatment

Oct 2, 2026 · @amitesh

Scene-by-scene treatments, one short film per phase rather than a single combined narrative.

## Intro: Tone & Look

Every film leaves one feeling: the quiet delight of earning something beautiful just by running. Warm, human, unpolished-real — ordinary Indian runners, real streets, real sweat. The animal cards appear as soft magical overlays, never loud game VFX. Natural light, early mornings and dusk, grounded documentary camera with one moment of wonder per film: the card reveal.

## Film 1 — The First Card

1. A young woman laces up outside a modest apartment, city still waking.
2. She runs through familiar lanes — chai stalls opening, dogs stretching. We stay with her breath and footfalls.
3. She pushes the last stretch, a real sprint, genuine effort on her face.
4. She stops, hands on knees, phone buzzes gently.
5. Card reveal: a cheetah mid-stride shimmers onto the screen, serial number stamping in. Her face breaks into a surprised grin.
6. End on her showing the phone to a friend at the stall. Cut to logo.

## Film 2 — The Traveller

1. A man steps off a train in Kerala, backpack on, new place, unfamiliar air.
2. Morning: he goes for an easy run along backwaters and coconut groves, just exploring.
3. He slows, taking in the greenery, not chasing pace.
4. Phone buzz — card reveal: a regional Kerala animal, a souvenir of the place, set name "Monsoon 2026".
5. He smiles, pockets the phone, keeps walking into the morning. Caption: every place you run leaves you something to keep.

## Film 3 — Many Runners, Many Animals

A montage across India, each runner earning a *different* animal, none better than another.

1. A 60-year-old uncle doing a steady, long endurance jog in Rajasthan — earns a majestic camel, age-graded so his effort counts fully.
2. A college kid sprinting a short burst in Delhi — a quick hare.
3. A woman in West Bengal, consistent all week — her animal glows with a streak finish.
4. A heavier runner, slow and unstoppable, earns a tortoise titled "Unstoppable".
5. Final shot: all their cards floating side by side, equal and distinct. Caption: different runs, different animals, no rankings.

## Film 4 — Night & Day

A split-world film about time of day.

1. Pre-dawn: a man runs empty blue-lit streets, birds just starting. Card reveal: a dawn songbird.
2. Hard cut to night: a woman runs a well-lit park after work, city glowing. Card reveal: an owl, eyes bright.
3. Intercut their strides to the same rhythm — two worlds, same app.
4. Caption: the hour you run changes what you find. End on both cards, sun and moon.

## Film 5 — Growth & The Share

1. Quick time-lapse of a runner showing up day after day — rain, tiredness, small wins.
2. One morning, a celebration animation: "Your cheetah is all grown up!" The baby animal is now a full adult. Real joy on her face.
3. She taps share — a clean poster with the animal and serial number, no map.
4. Cut to a friend across town getting the WhatsApp story, scanning the QR code.
5. Friend's phone: the public verify page confirms the card is real. Friend smiles, downloads the app.
6. End: the growth loop made visible. Caption: real runs, real cards, real proof.

## Closing, Taglines & Production Notes

**Closing frame:** a wall of wildly different cards from runners all over India, each with its serial number, settling into a grid — then the logo.

**Tagline directions:** "Run. Collect. Keep." / "Every run is worth something." / "The wild is yours to discover."

**Casting:** real runners of varied ages, body types, and regions — not models. Represent men, women, young and old equally.

**Locations across India:** Kerala backwaters, Rajasthan desert edges, a Bengal town, a Delhi or Mumbai street, a hill route. Shoot at golden hour and true night for the time-of-day films.
